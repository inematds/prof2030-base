Lesson 6 · 15 min

Rules with set dates: what applies now and what's coming

AI regulation feels like a distant topic, but part of it already applies today and part of it has a date on the calendar. Whoever helps companies doesn't need to be a lawyer; you need to know what applies now, what's coming, who it applies to and when to call legal. This lesson organizes all of that into a timeline you build for your own company.

Goal: build your company's compliance timeline: what applies now, what's coming, who it applies to and who owns each item.

02 · How to build the company's timeline

Four steps. Horizonte serves as the example.

1. List where AI is used

Start with the inventory: which tools use AI, what data they process and who the company sells to.

At Horizonte: WhatsApp customer service, conversation summaries, order app, email. Customers only in Brazil.

2. See what applies

LGPD: does it process personal data? Almost always yes. EU AI Act: does it sell or use AI in the European Union? At Horizonte, no.

Hiring, credit or education with AI? Flag them: these are high-risk areas under the AI Act and call for extra care in Brazil too.

3. Set the dates

Separate what already applies (act now), what has a date (plan), and what is under discussion (monitor).

Don't treat a bill as law: that spends energy in the wrong place.

4. Assign an owner and a next action

Every row needs someone who owns it and a next action, with a date.

Unsure whether a rule applies, about a contract, or about a legal basis? That's when you call legal. You organize; the legal opinion is theirs.

HorizonteLGPD · service summary · owner: Rafael with legal · stop sending without a contract within 30 days.

Simulation: illustrative example
  • LGPD · applies (customer data on WhatsApp) · already in force · owner: Carlos with legal · review the service summary in 30 days.
  • EU AI Act · doesn't apply today (no EU customers) · reassess if you export.
  • PL 2338/2023 · under discussion · monitor every quarter.
  • NIST AI RMF · voluntary · use the 4 functions as an internal checklist.
  • Treating a bill as law: PL 2338 is still in the Chamber of Deputies.
  • Thinking European law doesn't matter: it matters if the company sells or uses AI in the EU.
  • Giving a legal opinion: you organize facts and questions; legal is the one who concludes.

04 · Practice: your company's timeline

Use the inventory from lesson 2. No company? Use Horizonte.

10 min

Done when: the top of the template is filled in with the tools and the markets.

E2 · Applies or doesn't

15 min

For each rule in the template, answer "does it apply to us?" with the reason in one sentence.

Done when: all 5 rows have a yes or no and the reason, and the doubts went into "take to legal".

E3 · Owner and next action

10 min

Give an owner and a next action with a deadline to each row that applies. Set the next review for 3 months from now.

Done when: every row that applies has a name and a date, and the review is scheduled.
