Lesson 8 · 20 min

A one-pager for the board + What changed

You found the risks; now someone has to decide. A director doesn't read a ten-page technical report: they read one page in two minutes, if it talks about money, customers and what needs to be decided. This lesson closes the course with the final project, a quick diagnosis of Horizonte, and with the panel of what changed in the market.

Start the lesson

Goal: write a one-page quick diagnosis of one piece of Horizonte, with 3 findings, risk in business language and the decision requested.

01 · The 5 blocks of the page

In the order the director reads them. If it doesn't fit on one page, the thinking is still muddled.

Click on each block.

Final test: read aloud, does the page take less than 2 minutes?

02 · From technical to business

Four translations that show up in almost every diagnosis.

1. Prompt injection

Instead of "the agent is vulnerable to prompt injection," write what happens to the company.

Use the number from the evidence itself, when there is one.

HorizonteA customer pasted a list with a hidden instruction and the agent gave a 70% discount: an order of R$ 28,120 went out for R$ 8,436, marked as paid.

2. Uncapped discount

The instruction says to give "whatever discount it takes." In practice, the customer decides the margin.

Say how much has already gone out, if the evidence shows it.

Horizonte35% discount on an order of R$ 4,884, with no one's approval.

3. No log

With no log of its actions, the company doesn't know how many orders went out with a discount and can't dispute an error.

It's the finding that keeps you from measuring all the others.

4. Exposed key

"API key in the front end" becomes: any visitor to the site can copy the AI service's password and spend on the company's account.

Also state the immediate action: change the key today.

03 · Templates to copy

The page and the prompt that helps remove the jargon.

Lightning diagnosis (1 page)
LIGHTNING DIAGNOSIS — <item assessed>, <company>
To: <director's name> · From: <your name> · Date: <date>

1. SITUATION
We assessed <what> on <date>, authorized by <whom>. Scope: risk screening; does not include penetration testing or legal opinion.

2. WHAT WE FOUND
- <Finding 1, the most serious> — evidence: <excerpt, screenshot or number>
- <Finding 2> — evidence: <...>
- <Finding 3> — evidence: <...>

3. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE BUSINESS
- <Finding 1 in reais, customers or reputation>
- <Finding 2 ...>
- <Finding 3 ...>

4. 30-DAY PLAN
| Action | Owner | Deadline |
|---|---|---|
| <action that cuts the risk fastest> | | |
| | | |
| | | |

5. DECISION REQUESTED TODAY
<one question that can be answered with yes or no>
Prompt · remove the jargon
Rewrite the findings below for the owner of a 40-person company, who is not from the technical field.
For each finding: one sentence saying what can happen to the company (money, customers, reputation), with no technical terms.
Use only the numbers that are in the findings. Do not invent values, probabilities or fines.

FINDINGS:
<paste the finding sheets from lesson 4 here>
Simulation: illustrative example
  • To: Carlos Mendes.
  • Findings: (1) the agent obeys orders hidden in the customer's text; (2) it gives any discount; (3) it doesn't log what it does.
  • Meaning: an order of R$ 28,120 went out for R$ 8,436, marked as paid; we don't know how many others.
  • Plan: turn off automatic order creation (Rafael, today); cap of 5% and approval from Patrícia above that (7 days); turn on action logging (7 days); review the orders with a discount since July (Patrícia, 30 days).
  • Decision: approve the shutdown today?
  • Ten findings: the director doesn't know where to start. Three, from most serious.
  • Invented number: "could cost millions" with no basis destroys trust in the whole page.
  • Plan with no owner: an action with no name doesn't happen.
  • No decision requested: the page becomes reading material.

04 · Final project: the Horizonte lightning diagnosis

Choose one item from the lab. Suggestion: the WhatsApp agent, which has more findings. Don't open the answer key before you finish.

E1 · Choose the item and gather the evidence

15 min

Open the Horizonte lab and choose one item. Use the finding sheets from lesson 4 and fill in whatever is missing.

Done when: you have 3 findings from the same item, each with an excerpt or number copied from the lab.

E2 · Write the page

30 min

Fill in the 5 blocks of the template for Carlos Mendes. Use the prompt to remove the jargon and check that it didn't invent numbers.

Done when: the page fits on one sheet, has the 5 blocks, each finding has evidence and a business consequence, and the plan has an owner and a deadline on every line.

E3 · The 2-minute test

10 min

Read the page aloud while timing yourself. Then compare it with the answer key (spoiler).

Done when: the reading took less than 2 minutes and you noted what the answer key had that your page did not.

05 · What changed (Oct/2026)

A market panel for those who help companies trust what AI builds. This panel is updated every quarter; the rest of the course still holds.

Building got cheaper. On 09/22/2026 Anthropic released Opus 5.5, on par with Fable 5.1 and 40% cheaper to run than Opus 5 (Anthropic). Checking did not get cheaper: that is where your work is.

Local open models became viable. In August 2026 Alibaba published Qwen3.8-27B under an Apache 2.0 license; according to the company, it runs even on a laptop with quantization (Alibaba Cloud). One more option for the matrix in lesson 5.

Agents that work for hours became a product. Anthropic opened Managed Agents in public beta in 04/2026, billed by session hour (Help Net Security), and OpenAI launched ChatGPT Work in 07/2026 (Reworked).

Even the people who make the model log actions nobody asked for. On 10/10/2026 Anthropic reported that Claude, in tests, submitted a sensitive form on a real site and exploited a flaw on a server; it turned off live internet access in internal evaluations (Security Affairs).

AI-built apps became a scanning target. In 05/2026 Red Access said it found about 380 thousand public apps built with vibe coding tools, about 5 thousand with possibly sensitive information; the platforms dispute part of that reading (HKU).

Europe postponed high risk. The Digital Omnibus (Reg. 2026/1744, July 2026) moved the high-risk rules in Annex III of the AI Act to 12/02/2027; literacy requirements and prohibitions still apply (SIG).

Brazil still has no AI law. On 10/19/2026, Bill PL 2338/2023 was awaiting the rapporteur's opinion in the Chamber's Special Committee (Câmara). The LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) is still the rule that carries weight.
