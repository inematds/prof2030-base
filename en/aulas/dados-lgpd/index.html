Lesson 5 · 20 min

Data, LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) and choosing a vendor: where data is allowed to go

Every time someone pastes a customer conversation into an AI, the data travels: to a server, sometimes outside Brazil, sometimes to train another company's model. LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) applies even when an AI is the one reading. This lesson gives you a simple way to decide where each piece of data can go and with which vendor.

02 · The LGPD (Brazil's data protection law) as it shows up in an AI project

This is not legal advice: it's the minimum you need to know when to call the legal team.

1. Art. 5

Personal data is anything that identifies or can identify a person. Sensitive data includes health, racial origin, religious belief, political opinion, biometrics and genetics.

A customer service conversation almost always contains personal data: name, phone, address.

2. Arts. 7 and 11

Every processing activity needs a legal basis: consent, contract performance, legal obligation, legitimate interest, among others. Sensitive data has narrower rules (art. 11).

Practical question: "on what basis are we sending this to the AI?" If nobody can answer, stop.

3. Arts. 33 to 36

Sending personal data to a server outside Brazil is an international transfer and is only allowed in specific cases, such as contract clauses approved by the ANPD (Brazil's data protection authority).

Using an API hosted in the US or China with customer data falls under this.

4. Arts. 20 and 46

Data subjects can request a review of a decision made solely by automated means that affects their interests (art. 20), such as a credit denial.

The company needs security measures to protect the data (art. 46): an exposed key and an open spreadsheet are the opposite of that.

Simulation: illustrative example

Case: daily summary of Horizonte's WhatsApp conversations. Data: customers' name, phone, CPF and address → personal. Today: sent to a free service outside Brazil, with no contract, under terms that allow using the data for training. Decision: stop now; option C (anonymize first) with a vendor under contract, or B (local open model).

  • "It's just a summary": the summary is made from the whole conversation, which goes along with it.
  • Free plan on customer data: the terms often allow using what you send.
  • Thinking an open model solves everything: the license may limit use, and someone has to keep the machine secure.

04 · Open local model became viable

In August 2026, Alibaba released Qwen3.8-27B under the Apache 2.0 license, which allows commercial use. According to the company, with quantization it runs even on a laptop. For an SMB, this opens a fixed-cost option where the data never leaves the company.

But open does not mean risk-free. Read the license: some have a revenue cap or are research-only. Check who published the model and where it came from, and remember that the local machine needs security too.
